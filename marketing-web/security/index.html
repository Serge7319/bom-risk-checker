Security and data handling

Cadivor describes the following controls as part of its current application architecture. Contact Cadivor for details about your deployment and data-handling requirements.

AUTHENTICATION

Account access

Supabase-based authentication and role-aware application access support protected customer workspaces.

WORKSPACES

Data scoping

Workspace and analysis access is scoped to authorized users and organizations.

AUDITABILITY

Decision history

Decision records can preserve evidence, rationale, owner, timestamps, and approval history.

INTEGRATIONS

Server-side credentials

Supplier and AI integrations are designed to keep credentials on the server, outside the browser.

Cadivor does not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, or other formal certification on this page. Formal certifications and additional enterprise controls will be published if and when they are available.
