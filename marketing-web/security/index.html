Current controls

AUTHENTICATION

Account access

Supabase Auth handles account authentication. Protected routes use a verified user session and role-aware access checks.

WORKSPACES

Workspace scoping

Product APIs scope reports and decisions to the active workspace and authorized members. Shared links are limited to the specific content described by that feature.

CREDENTIALS

Server-side integrations

Supplier, AI, email, and payment credentials are configured server-side and are not sent to the browser by product routes.

PAYMENTS

Payment processing

Where billing is enabled, Stripe processes checkout and subscription payments. Cadivor does not store full card numbers.

Certification status: Cadivor does not currently claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, or another independent security certification. A SOC 2 readiness plan is in progress; it is not an audit or certification.

Retention and deletion

PUBLIC FREE AUDIT

Audit data and lead records

Shared summary links stop working after 30 days. Public audit rows are targeted for deletion after 30 days and email lead metadata after 180 days; the database cleanup schedule must be enabled in the production database for those deletion windows to run automatically. You can request earlier deletion by contacting legal@cadivor.com.

OPTIONAL ANALYTICS

First-party event data

Optional analytics are off unless a visitor opts in. Events exclude email, IP address, BOM contents, and component identifiers; event records are retained for up to 180 days.

CUSTOMER WORKSPACES

Account and BOM records

Workspace records are retained while the account is active. Users can delete supported saved BOM and report records in the product. Account-level deletion is handled through a verified request to legal@cadivor.com; backup and provider retention windows may apply.

REQUESTS

How to request deletion

Email legal@cadivor.com from the account address and identify the account or data you want removed. Cadivor verifies the request before acting and may retain limited records where required for legal, security, or payment obligations.
