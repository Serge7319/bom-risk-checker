    FAQ

    Questions hardware teams ask before starting.

    What BOM formats does Cadivor accept?

    CSV and Excel BOMs are supported, with normalization for common part-number, manufacturer, description, and quantity columns.

    Does Cadivor replace engineering judgment?

    No. Cadivor organizes evidence, identifies risks, and recommends actions while preserving human ownership and approval.

    Can decisions be audited later?

    Yes. Decision records preserve the evidence reviewed, rationale, owner, confidence, and approval history.

    Can Cadivor monitor approved BOMs?

    Yes. Monitoring tracks lifecycle, supply, and risk changes after a decision is approved.

    Security designed for engineering decision data.

    Cadivor is being built with tenant isolation, controlled access, traceability, and secure integrations in mind.

    Supabase-based authentication, role-aware entitlements, and row-level security support protected customer workspaces.

    Customer analyses, decision records, and collaboration data are scoped to authorized users and organizations.
