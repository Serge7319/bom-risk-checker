What the preview shows

PRIORITIZED REVIEW

High and medium risk first

Verified high and medium findings rank before exact-part verification gaps and verified low-risk rows. The preview labels unknown evidence instead of guessing a component risk.

SOURCE CONTEXT

See what suppliers reported

Review available lifecycle, inventory, lead-time, supplier-count, and risk-driver fields. Blank values stay blank when configured sources do not return usable evidence.

Limits and evidence

  • CSV or XLSX, up to 30 rows and 1 MB.
  • Two anonymous audits per connection in a rolling 24-hour window; supplier-enriched rows also count toward a shared daily capacity.
  • The free audit does not search for replacement parts.
  • Supplier snapshots vary by source, part, region, and time. They are not checkout inventory or a purchasing quote.
  • Do not upload regulated or export-controlled designs.
