CADIVOR · PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Learn what Cadivor collects, how information is used to operate the service, and how to contact us about privacy.

Information we collect

Cadivor may collect account information, uploaded BOM data, product usage information, and communications submitted to us. If you allow optional analytics, the marketing site and app record first-party product events such as page views, CTA clicks, audit completion, signup, analysis, and checkout milestones. Event records include limited UTM campaign tags and a browser-session identifier that is one-way hashed before storage. They do not include email addresses, IP addresses, BOM contents, or component part numbers. Analytics are off unless you choose to allow them; you can change this choice using Privacy choices on the marketing site or app.

How information is used

Information is used to provide, secure, improve, and support the service, communicate with customers, and comply with legal obligations. When enabled, marketing event data is used to understand aggregate conversion by traffic source and is retained for up to 180 days. When you submit a contact request, Cadivor stores the details you provide, your consent, and limited campaign attribution so we can respond and understand which sources bring requests. Do not include confidential BOM or security-sensitive data in the contact form.

Retention and deletion

Optional first-party analytics are retained for up to 180 days. Public audit share links stop working after 30 days; public audit result rows are targeted for deletion after 30 days and minimal email lead metadata after 180 days. Automatic database cleanup requires the production retention job to be enabled. Workspace BOMs and reports are retained while the account is active; users can delete supported saved records in the product or request account-level deletion by emailing legal@cadivor.com. Backups and legally required records may follow separate provider retention windows.

Data sharing

Cadivor does not sell personal information. Data may be shared with service providers required to operate the platform under appropriate safeguards. If a workspace owner configures an outgoing webhook, Cadivor sends the subscribed event fields (which can include part numbers, alert details, and evidence links) to that administrator-provided destination.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to legal@cadivor.com.
