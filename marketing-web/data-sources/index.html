DATA SOURCES & COVERAGE

Know what the evidence covers.

Cadivor combines exact-part supplier responses with engineering review tools. Coverage depends on the part, supplier, credentials, and fields each source returns.

Supplier sources

Product code supports authorized API connections to DigiKey, Mouser, Newark, and Nexar Supply (Octopart). A source is queried only when the deployment has the required credentials and the workflow supports that source. A name in this list does not mean every source is connected or returns a match for every component.

DISTRIBUTOR CATALOGS

DigiKey, Mouser, and Newark

Depending on the source response, Cadivor may show an exact manufacturer part match, stock, price, lead time, lifecycle label, datasheet, or product page. Missing fields remain unknown; they are not filled with estimates.

AGGREGATED DISCOVERY

Nexar Supply / Octopart

Where configured, Nexar can provide catalog and distributor offers for a part. Discovery results and listings are kept distinct from verified engineering compatibility.

Coverage varies by manufacturer, region, package, orderable SKU, and supplier catalog. Supplier values can change after a query and are not a stock reservation or purchasing quote.

Refresh behavior

ANALYSIS

On demand

A new analysis queries the configured sources used by that workflow. Previously saved evidence stays attached to its report so reviewers can see what supported the earlier decision.

OPTIONAL MONITORING

Opt-in scheduled checks

Scheduled monitoring is configured on a six-hour cadence for selected, opted-in workspaces and parts. Whether a check makes supplier calls depends on deployment readiness and configured source credentials. Cadivor does not continuously refresh every catalog or every field.

The Monitoring and Settings pages show operational readiness. A scheduled cadence does not guarantee that a supplier has new data or that every check produces an alert.

What Cadivor scores mean

BOM HEALTH · 0–100

A summary of component risk

When every component has verified supplier evidence and complete stock data, the BOM Health score is 100 minus the average component risk score, rounded to the nearest whole number. Higher means lower modeled supply risk. Cadivor withholds the numeric score when any row lacks required verification or risk-score data.

ALTERNATE COMPARISON CONFIDENCE · 0–100

Completeness of engineering evidence

This separate score is based on compared attributes, required family fields, and documented differences. Required-field coverage has more influence than optional fields; missing required pinout evidence caps the score. Catalog listing agreement does not count as an engineering match. A score is a review aid, not an approval or probability of field success.

Scores are workflow-specific. They should not be compared across different BOMs or treated as a substitute for datasheet review, electrical and mechanical validation, or qualified engineering approval.

Known limits

  • Supplier coverage is incomplete and differs between components and regions.
  • Stock, price, lead time, and lifecycle fields may be absent, stale, or inconsistent between sources.
  • RoHS, REACH, PFAS, product change notices, last-time-buy dates, and end-of-life dates are not universally available from connected sources. Cadivor shows these only when a component-specific source record is attached; missing data stays unknown.
  • Exact manufacturer and part-number identity must be checked; a search result alone does not establish interchangeability.
  • Cadivor does not replace manufacturer notices, distributor confirmation, design validation, or purchasing review.
  • Illustrative website samples use example quantities and workflow outcomes; they are not customer reports or evidence of live coverage.
