How the workflow works

Move from the uploaded parts list to an owned, evidence-linked action.

01 · Upload and validate

Check what Cadivor will analyze

Upload a CSV or Excel BOM, review suggested column roles and examples, correct row issues, then re-check before analysis. Part number and quantity are required. Duplicate part numbers are shown and combined visibly.

02 · Review evidence

Understand why a part is flagged

Inspect lifecycle, schedule, and supply signals with their source and identity context. A provider failure is shown separately from a successful search that found no supplier data. Unknown lifecycle does not mean active.

03 · Compare and qualify

Review suppliers and alternatives

Compare available source evidence and candidate parts. Similarity is a starting point: electrical, package, lifecycle, sourcing, and design fit still need engineering verification. “Hold for review” means the candidate is not approved.

04 · Record a decision

Keep the rationale with the evidence

Save the chosen action, supporting evidence, owner, and approval state. Cadivor organizes the record; an authorized engineer remains responsible for qualification and release approval.

05 · Revise and manage saved BOMs

Keep earlier reports intact

Rename a project or BOM, move a BOM between projects, or delete it. Editing parts creates a new saved revision; the original report remains available, and approved replacement evidence stays linked to its decision.

06 · Monitor selected parts

Review new evidence when checks run

Refresh supplier data manually for exact part numbers through configured distributors. Scheduled checks run every six hours for opted-in workspaces when scheduling and supplier refresh are enabled. Email alerts also require user opt-in, a verified account email, and enabled delivery.
